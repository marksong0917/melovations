Why Melovations

One team for your whole interior

Melovations Enterprises Inc. was founded in 2010 by Roy Melo in Bradford West Gwillimbury, and has been a trusted name in York Region ever since. We specialize in complete interior remodeling — for homes and for commercial spaces.

Design, rendering, architectural drawings and custom millwork are handled in-house, so nothing gets lost between plan and finished room. We take on your project with professionalism, skill, precision and care — and we keep you informed at every step.
